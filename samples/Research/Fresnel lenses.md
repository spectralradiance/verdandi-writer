# Fresnel lenses

Invented by Augustin-Jean Fresnel in the 1820s. Concentric rings of prisms bend light into a parallel beam, so a lens can be far thinner and lighter than a single solid lens of the same power.

Orders run from first (largest, for coastal lights) to sixth (harbours). A first-order lens can stand taller than a person.

> [!tip] For the novel
> The lens as a "machine that is also a faith": it does nothing but bend light, perfectly, for a century.
