# Lighthouses

Notes for [[Salt and Signal]].

- Keepers usually worked in pairs or as a family; logs were kept every watch.
- Lights were identified by their *characteristic*: the pattern of flashes and the period.
- Automation spread through the 20th century; many stations were automated by the 1980s and 1990s.
- See [[Fresnel lenses]] for the optics and [[Tide tables]] for the sea around Skerrow.

## Daily routine (from keepers' memoirs)

1. Trim wicks or replace mantles; refill oil.
2. Clean and polish the lens and brass.
3. Wind the clockwork that turns the lens.
4. Log the weather every few hours.
