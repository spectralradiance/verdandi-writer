## The Last Lamp

Paraffin vapour lamps (Chance Brothers, Hood pattern) were standard by the 1920s. Mantles failed often and keepers carried spares in tins.
Check: how many steps in a tower of about 30 m? Roughly 100–120 is plausible.

## The Wireless Man

Radio beacons for marine navigation were introduced in the early 1920s; ships took bearings on them with direction-finding receivers.

## Fog Week

Hand-cranked fog signals were kept as backups at many stations. A typical character: two blasts every minute or so; each station had its own pattern.

## What the Lens Knows

Fresnel lenses: concentric prisms bend light into a beam; a first-order lens can weigh several tonnes. See [[Fresnel lenses]].
