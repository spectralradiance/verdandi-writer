On the fifth night of the fog a ship called the Corran lost its bearings off the Skerrow reef, and Ilse listened to it lose them. Its operator sent the same question again and again, his fist getting looser and faster as the hours went on, until the dots and dashes ran together like a man talking in his sleep.

> [!notes]
> Pair with Fog Week: same night, two shores.

She could do nothing but answer. She gave him the bearings she had, which were not enough, and the weather, which he knew. At four in the morning his signal stopped. At half past four it began again, steadier, and she heard him send: LIGHT SEEN. HORN HEARD. THANK GOD FOR KEEPERS.

> [!outline]
> - The Corran, lost in fog
> - Ilse can only listen and answer
> - Silence, then: LIGHT SEEN. HORN HEARD.
