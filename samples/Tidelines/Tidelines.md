## Low Water

The sea goes out the way a guest leaves:
slowly, then all at once,
and the room it leaves behind
is full of things it never meant to show you.

Bladderwrack. A boot. The ribs of a boat
that was somebody's whole life once.

## Keeper

He winds the clock that turns the light that turns
the dark into a thing that can be crossed.
He does not ask what any sailor earns
or where they're bound, or what they've won or lost.

He only asks the oil to last the night,
the glass to hold, the wind to keep its word.

## Spring Tide

Twice a month the moon and sun agree
and pull together, and the water climbs
higher than it has any right to,
into the fields, the lanes, the church porch,
as though it had been invited.
