# Every Map Chooses

A map the size of the territory would be useless, and so every map is a decision about what not to show. The roads but not the hedges. The rivers but not the depth of them. The towns but not who lives there.

# The Coastline Problem

Measure a coastline with a long ruler and you get one length; measure it with a short one and you get a longer one, because the short ruler follows every cove and inlet the long one strides across. There is no true length, only the length at a given scale.

# The Map at the Front of the Book

Fantasy novels open with maps, and the maps make a promise: this world is real enough to survey. The best of them leave blank spaces at the edges. The blank is where the story might still go.
